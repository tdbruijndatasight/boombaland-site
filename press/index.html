Press kit

Boombaland is a bomb-blasting arcade game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Drop a bomb, run, and watch the board explode: battle brutal bosses, unlock six heroes with their own superpowers, and solve castle puzzles across 25 levels.

Fact sheet

Game
Boombaland
Developer
DataSight (Tom de Bruijn), the Netherlands
Release date
19 November 2026
Platforms
iPhone, iPad and Android
Price
Free to play, with optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded video ads
Genre
Bomb-blasting arcade game with bosses and puzzles
Content
25 levels across the floating forest and Castle Road, 6 heroes, bosses including the Thorn Guard, the Rage Tower and an enemy airship, story comics between chapters, 4 difficulties
Chests
No loot boxes: every chest shows the item it guarantees and the chance of everything else
Website
www.boombaland.com
Press contact
support@boombaland.com
