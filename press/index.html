Press kit

Boombaland is a classic bomb-maze arcade game for iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. Place bombs, break crates, trap the enemies and get out before the blast. Play through a campaign with castles and bosses, unlock heroes with their own skills, and solve push-block puzzles along the way. Out now on the App Store and Google Play.
