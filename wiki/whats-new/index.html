    What's new

    Patch notes, newest first.

    Version 1.0.7

    • A fresh look: bigger blasts, score popups and a new health and score bar.
    • Three star goals in every level, such as “Win in 120 seconds” or “Defeat with a bomb chain”. See Levels.
    • Living arenas: trees sway, islands float, and each round has its own time of day.
    • A new Home screen with a level carousel under a blue sky.
    • Revive with full health, once per game, and a clearer defeat screen.
    • Replays now show bosses, effects and sound.
    • Many fixes: Daily Shop, buttons, popups and sign-in.

    The first release

    Boombaland is out on the App Store and Google Play.

    • 25 levels across Green Lands and Castle Road, each in Easy, Normal, Hard and Impossible.
    • Six heroes: Blom, Gusto, Frosta, Zappy, Ember and Banshee.
    • Bosses and special enemies: Grimdorn, Arko, Nimbo, Turto and Gardo.
    • Gear, the Blacksmith, chests that show what they guarantee, and the Lucky Chest.
    • Daily rewards, daily and weekly tasks, and achievements in Game Center and Google Play Games.

    Every number on this page comes from the game's own data, read from the release build. Something wrong? Mail support@boombaland.com.
