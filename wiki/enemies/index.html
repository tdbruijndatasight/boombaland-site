    Enemies and bosses

    Who you are up against in Green Lands and on Castle Road.

    Bombers

    Thorn Guard's bombers play by the same rules as you: they drop bombs, grab power-ups and can be blown up.

    Twig Scout

    Places 1 bomb, Quick on its feet.

    First seen on Level 1

    Burr Bomber

    Places 2 bombs.

    First seen on Level 3

    Bramble Lobber

    Places bombs, Throws bombs.

    First seen on Level 7

    The boss

    Thorn Guard

    Walk over the switch tiles to break its shield!

    Fights you on Levels 5, 10, 20, 25.

    • Aimed Throws
    • Ring Break

    Towers, the airship and the castle guards

    Rage Tower

    Awake from the start, it shoots arrow-bombs across the board, over stones but not crates. An arrow takes a third of your health. Bomb it from right next to it to bring it down!

    Takes 3 hits. Range 7 tiles. An arrow takes 34 of your 100 health.

    Airship

    Thorn Guard's flying warship. Its cannonballs explode the moment they land, so watch the red markers and step away!

    Shellback

    The castle's stubborn doorkeeper. It spins and lobs bombs around itself, and the marks show where they land. Its shell blocks every blast until it walks: hit it while it walks, a bomb in its path!

    Takes 2 hits.

    Cobblewatch

    The castle's spear guards. They walk round the outside of the yard, line up with you, flash a yellow ! and charge straight across in pairs. Step out of their lane, or bomb them: they never dodge.

    A spear hit takes 34 of your 100 health.
