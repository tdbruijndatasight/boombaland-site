    Energy, coins and gems

    The three things you collect and spend, and the 28-day login.

    Energy

    Energy: every level costs 10 energy. It refills by itself: +1 every minute, up to 100. You start with 100. Watching an ad gives 50 energy (up to 5 times a day).

    Coins

    Coins: you earn them by winning levels, and spend them on boosters in the Shop. A first win of a level gives 30 coins, a replay 8, plus 8 for every star. Coins also pay for gear levels and hero stars.

    Gems

    Gems are rare. Spend them on upgrades, revives, energy refills and boost bundles. Achievements pay gems, and so does the daily login on some days.
