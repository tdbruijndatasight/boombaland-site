    Heroes

    Six heroes, six superpowers. Every hero drops bombs like Blom, plus one skill of their own.

    Stars: making a hero stronger

    Text marked "In the game" is the game's own wording, word for word: the skill's description from the Heroes screen's (i) and the "Now" and "Next star" lines under the skill. The numbers are worked out the way the game works them out.

    Every hero goes from ★1 to ★8. A star costs portraits of that hero plus coins. Universal portraits stand in for any hero's portraits. Each new star makes the skill stronger (the numbers above show ★1 and ★8).
