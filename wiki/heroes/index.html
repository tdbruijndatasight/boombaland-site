    Heroes

    Six heroes, six superpowers. Every hero drops bombs like Blom, plus one skill of their own.

    Blom

    Bomb Dash: Blom dashes ahead and cannot be hit during the dash.

    How to get Blom: Blom is your first hero: you play as Blom from the start.

    Dash3 tiles; 4 tiles from ★5
    Cooldown ★1 → ★815 s → 8 s

    Gusto

    Gust: A blast of wind in front of Gusto pushes enemies and their bombs away.

    How to get Gusto: Win Level 5 to meet Gusto. That first win gives 10 Gusto portraits and starts a trial of 5 games as Gusto. Collect 20 portraits to unlock Gusto for good.

    Reach4 tiles
    Push2 tiles; 3 from ★4; 4 from ★7
    Shapestraight line; a 3-tile-wide cone from ★5
    Cooldown ★1 → ★818 s → 10 s

    Frosta

    Frost Ring: A ring of frost freezes every enemy near Frosta.

    How to get Frosta: Win Level 10 to meet Frosta. That first win gives 10 Frosta portraits and starts a trial of 5 games as Frosta. Collect 20 portraits to unlock Frosta for good.

    Ring2 tiles; 3 from ★5
    Freeze ★1 → ★82.25 s → 2.42 s (bosses: 50% of that)
    Cooldown ★1 → ★820 s → 12 s

    Zappy

    Storm Call: Lightning strikes the enemies nearest to Zappy.

    How to get Zappy: Win Level 15 to meet Zappy. That first win gives 10 Zappy portraits and starts a trial of 5 games as Zappy. Collect 20 portraits to unlock Zappy for good.

    Range6 tiles
    Strikes3; 4 from ★4; 5 from ★7
    Damage5 per strike
    Stun ★1 → ★81 s → 2 s
    Cooldown ★1 → ★822 s → 14 s

    Ember

    Ember Trail: For a while every tile Ember walks over catches fire.

    How to get Ember: Win Level 20 to meet Ember. That first win gives 10 Ember portraits and starts a trial of 5 games as Ember. Collect 20 portraits to unlock Ember for good.

    Trail ★1 → ★84 s → 8 s
    Burn ★1 → ★83 s → 5 s
    Damage5
    Cooldown ★1 → ★820 s → 12 s

    Banshee

    Phantom Form: Banshee floats over the map, bombs anywhere and cannot be hit for a while.

    How to get Banshee: Banshee is sold in the Shop: for 150 gems, or as an in-app purchase.

    Phantom Form ★1 → ★86 s → 9 s
    Cooldown ★1 → ★845 s → 30 s

    Stars: making a hero stronger

    Every hero goes from ★1 to ★8. A star costs portraits of that hero plus coins. Universal portraits stand in for any hero's portraits. Each new star makes the skill stronger (the numbers above show ★1 and ★8).

    StarPortraits needed
    ★27
    ★310
    ★415
    ★523
    ★633
    ★750
    ★876

    A boss level you have won before gives 3 portraits each time you win it again.

    Every number on this page comes from the game's own data for version 1.0. Something wrong? Mail support@boombaland.com.
