    Getting started

    Controls, your first levels, and tips straight from the game.

    The story

    Welcome to Boombaland! Thorn Guard has claimed it, and Blom needs your help. Your goal: defeat Thorn Guard and free Boombaland from his thorns.

    Controls

    Move with the joystick, tap the bomb button to drop a bomb, and tap the skill button to use your hero's skill. Joystick and button settings are under Settings › Controls.

    Your first steps

    • Your levels. Swipe to see them all: winning a level opens the next one.
    • Stars show how well you did: one for a win, one for a win without a hit, one for the level's goal.
    • Pick Easy or Normal. Win a level to open Hard mode, then Impossible mode.
    • Energy: every level costs 10 energy. It refills by itself: +1 every minute, up to 100.
    • Boosters, chests and bundles, all on one page. Pick your boosters before a level. Tap back to return to camp.

    Tips from the game

    • Walk into a bomb to kick it away.
    • Ouch! In this tutorial bombs don't hurt you. But watch out: once we start, they do!
    • Careful: no fence on open edges. Falling off ends your run!
    • Tiles here can collapse at random
    • You found a key! Keys open the chest of their colour. Tap here to open your chests.
    • Daily and Weekly tasks are open! Finish tasks for points, and fill the bar to open its chests.

    Every number on this page comes from the game's own data for version 1.0. Something wrong? Mail support@boombaland.com.
