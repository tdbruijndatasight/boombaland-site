    Gear

    Boots, Gloves and a Helmet: three slots, nine looks, five rarities.

    New: Gear! You found your first piece. Wear Boots, Gloves and a Helmet to run faster, blast harder and stay safe. Equip them here and LEVEL UP them with coins; a card's colour is its rarity. Merge 3 pieces of one slot into a stronger one.

    Rarities and level caps

    Gear levels belong to the slot, not to the piece: level up your Boots once and every pair you wear counts at that level, up to the cap of the piece's rarity.

    The Blacksmith: merging

    Merge 3 pieces into one piece of the next rarity. Common, Uncommon pieces: any 3 pieces of the same slot and rarity will do (the first one you pick sets the look). From Rare up, the 3 pieces must be identical (same slot, look and rarity). Legendary is the top rarity and does not merge.

    Every number on this page comes from the game's own data for version 1.0. Something wrong? Mail support@boombaland.com.
