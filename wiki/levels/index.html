    Worlds and levels

    25 levels: Green Lands and Castle Road. Your goal: defeat Thorn Guard and free Boombaland from his thorns.

    Every level has four difficulties: Easy, Normal, Hard and Impossible. Pick Easy or Normal. Win a level to open Hard mode, then Impossible mode. Stars show how well you did: one for a win, one for a win without a hit, one for the level's goal.

    Level 21: Cloud Stones

    • Stars: ★ Win · ★ Win without taking a hit · ★ Clear every stage within 130 seconds
    • Castle puzzle: 3 stages. Stage 1: Gate opens: build the two-tile bridge and push a block onto the plate. Then walk out the east gate. · Stage 2: Gate opens: push a block onto each plate and defeat both guards. The gate is on the east wall. · Stage 3: Gate opens: build the three-tile causeway, push a block onto the far plate, defeat the guards.

    Level 22: Keeper's Locks

    • Stars: ★ Win · ★ Win without taking a hit · ★ Clear every stage within 88 seconds
    • Castle puzzle: 3 stages. Stage 1: Gate opens: hold all three locks down with blocks at once. Then walk out the east gate. · Stage 2: Gate opens: hold all three locks down and defeat the guard. · Stage 3: Gate opens: fill the two-tile bridge, hold both locks down and defeat both guards.

    Level 23: Shellfire Yard

    • Stars: ★ Win · ★ Win without taking a hit · ★ Clear every stage within 169 seconds
    • Castle puzzle: 3 stages. Stage 1: Gate opens: defeat the Shellback. Hit it while it walks. · Stage 2: Gate opens: a block on both plates and both Shellbacks defeated. · Stage 3: Gate opens: a block on the plate, the moat bridged and both Shellbacks defeated.

    Level 24: The Last Bell

    • Stars: ★ Win · ★ Win without taking a hit · ★ Clear every stage within 232 seconds
    • Castle puzzle: 3 stages. Stage 1: Gate opens: step on the glowing rune to ring the bell. Its gate opens briefly. Defeat the guard. · Stage 2: Gate opens: step on glowing runes to ring the bell and open their colour's gate. Defeat both guards. · Stage 3: Gate opens: step on glowing runes to ring the bell and open their colour's gate. Defeat 3 guards.

    Level 25: Castle Heart

    • Stars: ★ Win · ★ Win without taking a hit · ★ Win using at most 70 bombs
    • Also here: Thorn Guard
    • Castle puzzle: 2 stages. Stage 1: Main gate opens: defeat the guards and towers, a block on the plate, a bridged gap, both runes lit. · Stage 2: Throne gate opens: destroy the two towers on the sides, the guards and both Shellbacks, then defeat Thorn Guard.
