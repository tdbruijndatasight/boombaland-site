Ready, Set, BOOM!

Drop a bomb, run, and watch the board explode. Battle brutal bosses and unlock epic heroes in bright 3D adventures.

Classic bomb battles with a fresh new twist.

For iPhone, iPad and Android. Free to play.

Drop. Run. Boom!

Simple touch controls: move, drop a bomb, use your skill. Kick bombs, grab power-ups and plan every blast.

Unlock epic heroes

  • Blom dashes through danger with Bomb Dash
  • Gusto blows enemies and their bombs away with Gust
  • Frosta freezes everyone nearby with Frost Ring
  • Zappy calls lightning down with Storm Call
  • Ember sets the ground on fire with Ember Trail
  • Banshee floats over the map with Phantom Form

Star up every hero and make each skill stronger.

Battle brutal bosses

  • The Thorn Guard hides behind a thorn shield, rains bomb barrages and gets faster when he is enraged
  • The Rage Tower wakes up and fires arrow-bombs across the board
  • An enemy airship drops in with its crew and cannons
  • Your goal: defeat Thorn Guard and free Boombaland from his thorns

Castle puzzles

25 levels across the floating forest and Castle Road, each with its own twist: bridges, switches, towers, timed gates and push-block puzzles. Story comics between chapters, and 4 difficulties, from Easy to Impossible, each with its own three stars on every level.

No loot boxes

Every chest shows the item it guarantees, and the chance of everything else, before you open it. No mystery, no surprises.

Earn new heroes, gear, power-ups and Codex entries as you play. Equip boots, gloves and helmets, level them up and merge them at the Blacksmith.
