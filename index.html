The game

What is Boombaland?

Boombaland is a classic bomb-maze arcade game for iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. You drop bombs, run from the blasts, break crates, grab power-ups and outsmart enemies on grid boards, with bosses and push-block puzzles along the way.

  • 25 levels
  • 6 heroes
  • 4 difficulties
  • 0 loot boxes

Drop. Run. BOOM!

Simple touch controls: move, drop a bomb, use your skill. Kick bombs, grab power-ups and plan every blast, then get out before it goes off.

Levels that move

25 levels across Green Lands and Castle Road, each with its own twist: bridges, switches, towers, conveyor belts and timed gates. Story comics play between the chapters.

Castle puzzles

On Castle Road, push blocks into the gaps to cross. Four difficulties, from Easy to Impossible, each with its own three stars on every level.

No loot boxes

Every chest shows the item it guarantees, and the chance of everything else, before you open it. No mystery, no surprises.

Earn new heroes, gear, power-ups and Codex entries as you play. Equip boots, gloves and helmets, level them up and merge them at the Blacksmith.

Heroes

Six heroes, six skills

Every hero drops bombs. Each one has a skill of their own. Star up every hero and make each skill stronger.

  • Blom

    Bomb Dash

    Blom dashes ahead and cannot be hit during the dash.

  • Gusto

    Gust

    A blast of wind in front of Gusto pushes enemies and their bombs away.

  • Frosta

    Frost Ring

    A ring of frost freezes every enemy near Frosta.

  • Zappy

    Storm Call

    Lightning strikes the enemies nearest to Zappy.

  • Ember

    Ember Trail

    For a while every tile Ember walks over catches fire.

  • Banshee

    Phantom Form

    Banshee floats over the map, bombs anywhere and cannot be hit for a while.

The story

Stop Grimdorn

Your goal: defeat Grimdorn and free Boombaland from his thorns.

  • Grimdorn hides behind a thorn shield, rains bomb barrages and gets faster when he is enraged
  • Arko, the tower, shoots arrow-bombs across the board
  • Nimbo, Grimdorn's flying warship, fires cannonballs that explode the moment they land
  • On Castle Road, Turto blocks every blast with its shell until it walks

Good to know

What's in the game?

It has 25 levels in Green Lands and on Castle Road, six heroes (Blom, Gusto, Frosta, Zappy, Ember and Banshee), each with their own skill, and four difficulties from Easy to Impossible. Besides the Campaign there is the Training Ground: quick battles against CPU opponents. You play on your own, against computer-controlled enemies.

Is it free?

Boombaland is free to play. It offers optional in-app purchases. Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a reward. There are no loot boxes.

Is Boombaland out yet?

Yes. Boombaland is out now on the App Store and Google Play.

Who makes Boombaland?

DataSight, the one-person studio of Tom de Bruijn in the Netherlands.
