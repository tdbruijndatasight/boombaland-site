### 2.9 Ideas you send us on the website

On www.boombaland.com/development/ you can send us an idea for the game. This is separate from the game, and you don't
have to do it.

- **What we receive:** the title and text of your idea. If you choose to add them, we also receive your name and email
  address. We only accept a name or email address from people who tick "I am 16 or older". Younger players can send an
  idea without them.
- **Why:** to read and consider your idea. If you gave your email address, we use it to reply, for example to talk
  about the details if we like the idea. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR article 6(1)(a)), which you give with the
  tick box on the form.
- **How it reaches us:** the form is sent by **[Web3Forms / Formspree — fill in the service chosen at go-live]**, which
  passes it on to our mailbox. That service handles the submission under its own privacy policy as our processor
  **[and is based in the United States: fill in the transfer safeguard it offers, e.g. the EU-U.S. Data Privacy
  Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses]**. We keep ideas in our own mailbox. We do not put them in the game's
  data.
- **How long:** we delete an idea, and any name and email address that came with it, **12 months after our last
  contact about it**. If we never replied, that is 12 months after you sent it. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we
  do.
- **Publication:** we never publish your idea, your name or your email without asking you first. If we use an idea in
  the game, we ask whether, and how, you would like to be credited.
- **Your rights:** you can withdraw your consent, and ask to see, correct or delete what you sent, at any time by
  mailing support@boombaland.com (see section 7).

## Notes for the PO

- **Age:** the form asks "I am 16 or older" before it accepts contact details. 16 is the Dutch GDPR age of digital
  consent (UAVG article 5).
- **Ideas and ownership:** this subsection is about privacy only. Whether a submitted idea gives the sender any rights
  is a terms question, and is not covered here. A short line in the Terms ("ideas you send are given freely; we may use
  them without payment, and we will ask before naming you") is common practice. It needs your decision.
- **Processor:** pick the service in GO_LIVE.md step 7, then fill in the two bracketed parts above. Neither option
  processes the data in the EU (see GO_LIVE.md).
